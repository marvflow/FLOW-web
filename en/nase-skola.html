Classrooms at FLOW

Home classrooms, deep work and relax zones.

Children don't have to learn only at a desk. Classrooms include additional zones — for different types of work, different paces, different moods.

01 Main zone

Main classroom

The classic space for shared lessons, projects and discussions. Movable furniture lets us reshape the room for the activity.

02 Calm & reset

Relax zone

A place for breaks, reading and quieter pair work. Soft seating, pillows, books — a safe space to reset.

03 Focus

Deep work

A quiet zone for independent, focused work. Children learn to recognise when they need calm and take it.

Three zones combined with flexible furniture and 90-minute blocks let children and teachers dive deeper into a project and shift pace as needed.
