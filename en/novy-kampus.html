New campus FAQ

What parents ask.

Click a category to filter.

Is the building exclusive to the school, or shared?

The campus has its own separate entrance and reception. Children enter a space that is exclusively the school's — not shared with other tenants. Movement of children is controlled, the reception monitors arrivals and departures.

How is student movement controlled?

Arrivals and departures via a chip system + reception. Every child has a chip; parents can specify who is allowed to pick the child up. Arrival/departure notifications go to parents via push or SMS.

Do classrooms have enough daylight?

Yes — every classroom has large windows with outside access. The architect designed the space so that daylight is the primary source. Artificial lighting complements but doesn't dominate.

What about traffic and surrounding noise?

An acoustic study confirms classroom levels will be below 45 dB — the level of a quiet library. Acoustic wall and ceiling treatments, insulated windows, real-time monitoring.

What about air quality? Ventilation, CO₂?

Full ventilation with heat recovery and CO₂ monitoring in every classroom. Air is exchanged continuously, not based on opening a window. The hygiene authority has approved the system.

What about parking and drop-off?

K+R drop-off zone right at the campus for short stops. Parking in surrounding streets (time-limited zones — we're tracking). We're evaluating in-building garage spots for teachers and parents on subscription.

How does child check-in and check-out work?

Chip system at reception + parent notifications (app push or SMS). On morning arrival and afternoon pickup — you always know when the child entered or left, and with whom.

How will meals work?

On-site serving area in the campus, external supplier (we're selecting a partner with a focus on quality). We cover allergies, gluten-free, vegetarian and other dietary variants. Cost about 2,000 CZK/month.

Where will children move and exercise?

10 minutes' walk from the campus is a modern sports arena — our main PE space. Full equipment, courts, gymnastics and other sports. Plus our own outdoor space (garden) at the campus for daily movement and outdoor learning.

Do children have their own outdoor space?

Yes. Our own garden at the campus for regular outdoor time, project days, outdoor lessons. The Balabenka neighbourhood also has public parks we use for walks and outdoor programmes.

Will class sizes change?

Yes, we're moving the cap to a maximum of 16 children per class. This is a new limit by design — based on our pedagogical philosophy and the capacity of the space.

Will the same teaching team stay?

Yes. The teachers who currently lead classrooms move with us to the new campus. Team stability matters to us as much as space stability.

Is this a long-term home, or will you move again?

The lease is 10 years with a 5+5 option. We plan to stay long-term — the campus is designed to last and grow with us.

Will tuition change at the new campus?

No increase. Tuition stays at 180,000 CZK per year, 10% sibling discount. Details on the Tuition & fees page.

Can we visit the new campus in person?

Yes. We're preparing parent tour dates during summer 2026 (after construction completes). For serious applicants for 2026/27 we offer individual tours earlier — write to campus@skolaflow.cz.

What if I hear negative speculation about the campus?

Rumours circulate around any major change. If you hear something and want it from the source — write or call. The school's leadership responds directly, without intermediaries.

Is there a dedicated contact for campus questions?

Yes: campus@skolaflow.cz — all campus-specific questions go directly to the build-out team. Response within one business day.

How will Little FLOW (Kindergarten) work at the new campus?

Little FLOW has a separate entrance and operation in the campus. Capacity 24 children, also opening September 2026. Details on the Little FLOW page.
