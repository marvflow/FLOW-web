Why FLOW

Why parents choose FLOW

01

Small classes

Max 16 students per class — more space for questions, mistakes and growth. Teachers know every student.

02

Daily English

Children hear and use English throughout the week — in projects, sport and casual communication. Cambridge exams on-site.

03

Project-based learning

Children explore, create and learn to think in context — not memorise facts.

04

Safe environment

Teachers know every child by name. We build on trust, relationships and regular feedback — for children and parents alike.
