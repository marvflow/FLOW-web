Meet our team

FLOW School is led by a team of experts. In every classroom you will find a lead teacher, a teaching assistant, and a native English speaker, supported by a psychologist and speech therapist — ensuring comprehensive support for all aspects of education and student development.

Open Positions

Our team

After-school programmes and care are run by our education team, which organises various after-school clubs on school premises, expanding the educational spectrum beyond the academic framework.

They are supported by an ICT coordinator and external programming teachers who bring digital literacy and coding skills into the classroom. This unique team composition enables us to deliver an educational programme based on projects and the "learning by doing" approach, supporting children in active learning through experimentation, making mistakes, and learning from them together with our specialists. In this way we prepare our students for the challenges of the 21st century, developing their critical thinking, independence and adaptability, and creating an environment where every child can grow and discover their full potential.
