Lower secondary at FLOW · Grades 6–9

Your child’s school ends after Grade 5? Continue with us.

At FLOW’s lower secondary level, your child will find small classes, extended English, Spanish and focused preparation for the entrance exams in Czech and mathematics. In our new campus at Balabenka, with a tram stop right at the entrance.

Why FLOW

Six reasons to choose FLOW for Grades 6–9.

Lower secondary decides which secondary school a child gets into. That is why we build on what really helps during those four years.

01

Entrance exam preparation

Focused preparation for the national CERMAT entrance exam in Czech and mathematics, led by teachers who specialise in these subjects.

02

Small classes

A maximum of 16 children per class. Teachers know every child, see where the gaps are and work with each child individually.

03

Extended English

English every day with English teachers. We are a Cambridge English partner, and Cambridge exam preparation takes place at school.

04

Spanish as a second language

A second foreign language taught by our Spanish teacher. One more language that opens doors for your child.

05

Subject specialists

Our team is made up of teachers and subject experts. Every subject is taught by someone who knows it in depth.

06

New campus at Balabenka

Modern premises at the heart of Prague 9. A tram stop at the entrance, the metro a 10-minute walk away.

Programme

What lower secondary looks like at FLOW.

Teaching follows an MŠMT-accredited curriculum, with an emphasis on what children need for the entrance exams and for life.

  • Czech and mathematicsSystematic preparation for the national entrance exam in both subjects.
  • EnglishEvery day, with English teachers and Cambridge exam preparation.
  • SpanishSecond foreign language.
  • Computer scienceProgramming and digital creation: HTML/CSS/JS, Roblox Studio, Unity.
  • Project-based learning5 major projects a year. Children tackle real problems, work in teams and present their results.
  • Safety and wellbeingAn in-house special educator, a respectful approach and clear rules.

Joining FLOW

Moving up to Grade 6, step by step.

No pressure, and time to decide. For you and for your child.

01

Get to know us

Come to an Open Day, join a School Tour with leadership, or book an online meeting with the school leadership.

02

Trial days

Your child spends 3 days in a regular class of their year group, free of charge and with no obligation. How trial days work.

03

Conversation and agreement

We talk about how your child got on, what they need and what their goals are. The contract is signed only once we have both agreed.

04

Starting at FLOW

Most often in September, but children can also join during the school year. Fees and tuition.
