What Little FLOW does differently

Six pillars of Little FLOW.

Three pillars of pedagogy plus three pillars of environment and relationships. Together they form a unique kindergarten.

01

Project-based learning in practice

Children explore a topic in depth — through movement, creation, discussion and collaboration. Example: Our city — a walk to the bakery, building a neighbourhood model, mini exhibition for parents.

02

English every day

Present in morning rituals, songs and games. Example: morning circle in Czech, snack in English — always a natural context, never forced.

03

Technology without screens

Bee-Bots, Ozo-Bots and Cubetto — physical programmable toys. Hands-on, tied to projects. No passive content consumption.

04

Max 8 children, 2 adults

A teacher and assistant every day. A small group — every child gets attention. Calm environment, space for all emotions.

05

Smooth transition to FLOW Elementary

Joint events, portfolios, classroom visits. Children know their future teachers and spaces long before starting 1st grade.

06

Partnership with parents

Twigsee app, regular photos and messages from the teacher, child portfolio, community events for the whole family.

What's included in tuition
  • Full educational programme and daily care provided by the school's teaching team
  • Snacks and drinking water throughout the day
  • Standard learning materials and activities used during teaching
  • Regular communication with parents and community events at school
Additional services and costs
  • Lunches are billed separately
  • Extracurricular clubs and optional after-school activities are paid separately, based on the school's current offering
  • Some special events and trips (e.g. theatre, zoo, museums, transport or workshops) may require an additional contribution from parents

For full transparency, the class may collect a shared contribution at the start of the school year for small events and trips during the year. The school or class teachers will keep parents informed about how these funds are used and share a cost overview.

We'll go through exact terms, payment methods and the contract at the personal meeting.

How to start

Admission in three steps.

A simple process, no bureaucracy. We want to get to know you — not bury you in forms.

01

Personal meeting

Book a meeting with the leadership. We'll walk through the admission process, contract, logistics and answer everything you want to know.

Joining without stress

Adaptation tailored to every child.

There's no fixed schedule — we adapt to the child, not the other way around. Parents are part of the process from day one.

We start before September

Meetings, events and school visits already during the summer — the child arrives in September to a familiar environment.

3–4 weeks of adaptation

Adaptation typically takes 3–4 weeks, but without pressure. Every child has their own pace.

Close communication
