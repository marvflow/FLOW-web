Founders' School

We teach children to take things into their own hands.

FLOW was founded by people who built things from zero themselves. That's exactly what we want to pass on to the children: the ability to create, not just reproduce. To lead, not just listen. To learn from mistakes, not fear them.

  • Take things into your own hands: initiative over passivity
  • Do, create, present: 10 Demo Days per year, where children present their projects to parents
  • Solve real problems in context: in projects, not just in textbooks
  • A mistake is a step forward: not something to fear
  • Leadership: lead a team, take responsibility — from 1st grade

Our method

Three pillars we build on

I want every child leaving FLOW to feel "I can take things into my own hands" — whatever they choose to do next.

— Marv Shamma, FLOW Founder
01 Confidence

Confidence to create

The child knows they can make, solve, build something. Instead of "I don't know, I can't," they say "I'll try, I'll show you."

02 Team & leadership

Collaborate and lead

The child can work in a team — listen, suggest, hit a deadline. And they can lead a team, not only be led.

03 Autonomy

Learn independently

The child knows where to find information, how to verify it, how to ask. School ends; learning is for life.
