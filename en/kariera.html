What we offer

A partnership, not a job.

01 · Pay

Fair money, not the minimum

Pay reflects experience and role — not pay grids. For higher-responsibility roles (class teacher, director) there are also bonus components tied to school outcomes.

02 · Development

In-house training and a development budget

Methodology, leadership, child psychology, talent development — in-house and external. Study leave and a financial contribution toward further education.

03 · Team culture

Open, flat, collegial

No micromanagement. Decisions are made in the team, we support experiments and open feedback. Marv (the founder) is reachable and listens.

04 · Backup

A specialist support team

Child psychologist, methodologist, speech therapist, external experts. An assistant in every classroom and a native English speaker part-time. You're not on your own.

05 · Sick days + holidays

Sick days above the statutory minimum

Sick days as standard, holiday rhythm matching state schools. Health and recovery are foundational — nobody benefits from burnt-out teachers.

06 · Tuition discount

Your children at FLOW at a discount

If you have children in kindergarten or primary, they can attend FLOW at a significant tuition discount. The exact amount is agreed individually.

Why FLOW

Our vision and how we live it.

Our vision

A happy, fulfilled person ready for life in the 21st century. Readiness, in our view, means being able to adapt quickly to a changing world full of new challenges.

Our goal is a child who can say:

"I don't know what the future holds — but I have a mindset that helps me handle even the unknown."

If that resonates with you, we're on the same wavelength.

How we do it

The school currently runs grades 1–7 and is growing organically. Class capacity is up to 16 children. The teaching team always works as teacher + shared assistant or native speaker.

We have a specialist support team available — child psychologist, education methodologist, speech therapist. We're growing partnerships with external experts on specific competencies (Kiddům, Supercreators).

From September 2026 we're moving to the new campus in Prague 9 Balabenka.
