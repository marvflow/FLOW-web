Enrolment at FLOW is different. 30–60 minutes of playful assessment with your child, a conversation with school leadership — and you leave with concrete feedback about your child and a clear sense of whether FLOW is right for you.

What to bring

Birth certificate + ID

Your child's birth certificate and both parents' IDs. Anything else we'll confirm by email.

Length

30–60 minutes

Playful assessment with the child + a conversation with school leadership. No stress, no tests.

Process

How the process works

Four clear steps. No pressure, with time for both sides to decide.

01

Get to know each other

Application + an initial meeting with school leadership, or an Open Day. You get to know FLOW; we get to know you.

02

Experience morning (optional)

Your child spends a morning with our first-graders. A real experience before you decide.

03

Assessment & conversation

30–60 minutes: a teacher with the child (play), school leadership with you (a conversation about expectations). You'll get concrete feedback.

04

Enrolment day

Formal enrolment with paperwork. If demand exceeds capacity, a transparent lottery decides. Document details will arrive by email.

Assessment

How enrolment works with us

A structured methodology in playful form. For each of six areas we have a specific activity — and a clear view of what we're observing in the child.

01
Fine-motor skills
🎨
Activity Drawing a house, a figure, outlines.
👁️
What we observe Grip, line, fluency, spatial orientation of the hand.
02
Spatial reasoning
🧱
Activity Building with blocks, copying a pattern.
👁️
What we observe 3D perception, planning, relationships between objects.
03
Visual and auditory perception
🔍
Activity Spot the difference between pictures, repeat a rhythm.
👁️
What we observe Attention to detail, discrimination, sequencing.
04
Communication
💬
Activity Telling a story about the weekend, describing a picture.
👁️
What we observe Vocabulary, sentence structure, fluency of expression.
05
Numeracy
🐚
Activity Counting shells, sorting by size.
👁️
What we observe Number sense, quantity, more/less relationships.
06
General knowledge
🌳
Activity Pictures and questions about the world and the seasons.
👁️
What we observe Orientation in basic concepts, curiosity.

The methodology is based on a validated school diagnostic tool — the child experiences a playful format and we get a structured profile. After 30–60 minutes you leave with concrete feedback, regardless of the admission decision.
