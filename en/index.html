Our pillars

Four reasons parents choose FLOW.

Details under each pillar. Capacity is limited — apply early.

01

Project-based learning

10+ real-world projects per year. Children create, solve problems, present. Learning by doing, not memorising for tests.
02

Extended English

Daily English from 1st grade with native speakers. Cambridge YLE preparation on-site.
03

Programming

Minimum screen time. We teach children to create with technology, not just consume it.
04

Safety

On-site special educator, restorative dialogues, buddy system. No punishment — built on respect.
