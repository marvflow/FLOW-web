Fees FAQ

Parents' most common questions.

Can I pay monthly instead of by semester?

Yes. The standard rhythm is two semester instalments (September + February), but by agreement with school leadership we can set up a monthly payment plan or another individual arrangement. It's not a problem — the important thing is that the cash flow works for you.

Is tuition refunded for illness or leaving mid-year?

Short-term illness (up to a few weeks) isn't deducted — the place in class, the teacher and all resources for the child remain. If a child leaves during the school year, the prorated share of tuition for unattended months is refunded. Details are set out in the contract.

Are there any joining or enrolment fees?

No. No enrolment fee, no joining fees. The only optional fee outside tuition that relates to enrolment is the school-readiness assessment (CZK 3,000) — and that's entirely optional.

What if I lose my job or our financial situation changes mid-year?

Talk to us early. It's not in our interest for a child to have to leave because of a short-term setback. We can agree on changes to the payment schedule, a deferral, or an individual solution. Trust here is mutual.

Can I arrange payment individually?

Yes. School leadership is open to this — we can agree on an individual deadline, a custom payment schedule, or another arrangement. Just get in touch — we're not a bank, we're a school.
