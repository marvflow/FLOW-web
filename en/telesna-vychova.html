Physical education and movement at FLOW

A strong body, a confident mind, healthy habits for life.

Our approach to PE differs sharply from traditional school lessons. The goal isn't athletic performance, it's holistic development of the child through movement — coordination, social skills, healthy habits.

Gym 10-min walk from campus 90-minute blocks Health-PE principles Monthly PE done differently
Philosophy and pillars

Four pillars we build on.

The aim isn't who's the fastest, it's who feels good moving. We build broad physical development, social skills and a lifelong relationship with an active life.

01

Physical development

Coordination, speed, strength, endurance, flexibility, motor skills. A broad foundation for any sport — and for everyday life.

02

Social skills

Fair play, respect, cooperation, responsibility. Sport as a training ground for relationships and character.

03

A wide range of sports

Team and individual — ball games, athletics, gymnastics, martial arts. Every child finds something they enjoy.

04

Tournaments and competitions

Growing competitive spirit, teamwork, handling real conditions. No pressure on the result — focus on the experience.

How we teach

Blocks, pupil participation, health principles.

Organisation

90-minute blocks with pupil participation

Lessons run in two-hour blocks. A short theoretical intro — pupils know why, not just how. Younger pupils actively prepare warm-ups. Older pupils design exercises and games themselves.

  • Younger pupils: actively prepare warm-ups
  • Older pupils: design their own exercises and games
  • Movement integrated into whole-school projects
Health PE

A body that lasts a lifetime

We teach children correct posture and mindful movement. Not just what to do, but how to do it — so the body serves them for decades without injury.

  • Deep stabilisation system + posture
  • Correcting movement patterns
  • Injury-prevention principles + ergonomics
  • Efficient movement for life
12× trips a year
Whole-school sports trips

A new sport each month — one most schools can't offer.

Non-traditional sports outside the school gym — skiing, snowboarding, ropes courses, climbing, laser tag. Practical use of PE skills and memories for a lifetime.
