Why I founded FLOW and how I see education

When my sons were born, I was looking for a school where they could grow in maths and in emotional intelligence alike. I didn't find one — so I built it. A future-ready school for children whose future we cannot predict.

Why FLOW bets on project-based learning

"You just have to learn maths, otherwise you'll achieve nothing." At FLOW we approach it differently. Children rescue whales, design ad campaigns, make films. Learning by doing — because we learn most through practice.
